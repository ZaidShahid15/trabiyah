Purposeful Nature

The anti-screen
for intentional families.

More Details

Frequently Asked Questions.

Clear answers for intentional parents.

How does the display work? Is it an iPad?

No. Tarbiyah uses a matte anti-glare e-ink panel — similar to a Kindle — that reflects ambient light instead of emitting it. This means no blue light, no glare, and a paper-like appearance that looks beautiful in any lighting condition in the home.

How are the prayer times calculated?

Tarbiyah auto-syncs to your home coordinates and supports ISNA (North America), Umm al-Qura, MWL, and custom mosque offset formulas — all configurable from the companion app.

Do I need a subscription to use the app or device?

No. Tarbiyah works fully without any subscription. Core features — prayer times, habit tracking, family board — are included for life. Optional premium personalization features may be introduced later but will never lock core functionality.

Where is the physical device crafted?

Tarbiyah bezels are crafted from sustainably harvested solid hardwood — American Walnut, light Natural White-Oak, and a Travertine stone edition. Each piece is finished by hand and inspected before shipping.

What is the return policy and warranty?

Founding family pre-orders are fully refundable before the shipping date. All devices come with a 12-month manufacturer warranty covering hardware defects.
